# Levels of Implication Framework
## "How am I implicated? What can I do?"

---

## The Core Insight

**Old model:**
- Here's a crisis
- Here's what you can do
- (Usually: donate $5)

**Problem:**
- Feels disconnected
- Doesn't explore complicity
- Oversimplifies action
- "Just send money" = disengagement

**New model:**
- Here's a crisis
- **Here's how you're implicated at different levels**
- Here's what you can do **at each level**

---

## The Framework

### For each Issue or Moment, we explore:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ INDIVIDUAL                                       │
│ How am I personally connected?                   │
│ What can I do in my daily life?                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FAMILY                                           │
│ How does this touch my family?                   │
│ What conversations can we have?                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ COMMUNITY                                        │
│ What's my local community's role?                │
│ What can we organize together?                   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CULTURE                                          │
│ How does my cultural background relate?          │
│ What traditions/values apply?                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ COUNTRY                                          │
│ What's my nation's role in this?                 │
│ What political action can I take?                │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ GLOBAL/SYSTEMIC                                  │
│ How do systems I participate in contribute?      │
│ What structural change is needed?                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Example 1: Gaza Humanitarian Crisis

### INDIVIDUAL
**How am I implicated?**
- Do I consume media that dehumanizes Palestinians?
- Have I examined my own biases about Israel/Palestine?
- What companies do I support with my purchases?
- Do I speak up when I see dehumanization?

**What can I do?**
- Educate myself on the history
- Examine my own prejudices
- Boycott companies funding the occupation (BDS)
- Have difficult conversations with friends/family
- Donate to medical relief

**Example actions:**
```
→ Read "The Hundred Years' War on Palestine"
→ Cancel SodaStream subscription (BDS target)
→ Talk to my uncle who says "they're all terrorists"
→ Donate $20/month to Medical Aid for Palestinians
```

### FAMILY
**How am I implicated?**
- Does my family have connections to Israel/Palestine?
- Do we have money invested in companies supporting occupation?
- What narratives were we taught growing up?
- How do we talk (or not talk) about this?

**What can I do?**
- Start conversations at dinner table
- Review family investments (retirement funds, etc.)
- Share what I'm learning
- Create space for different views

**Example actions:**
```
→ Ask parents about their 401k holdings
→ Suggest moving funds out of companies on BDS list
→ Share documentary with siblings
→ Weekly family check-in on the issue
```

### COMMUNITY
**How am I implicated?**
- Does my city have ties to Israeli military/tech?
- Does my university invest in occupation?
- Does my workplace sell to IDF?
- What's my synagogue/church/mosque saying?

**What can I do?**
- Organize community vigil
- Petition local institutions
- Coordinate with others
- Build local awareness

**Example actions:**
```
→ Weekly vigil at Federal Building (SF Pack)
→ Petition UC Berkeley to divest
→ Coordinate with 3 other local groups
→ Speaker series on Palestinian history
```

### CULTURE
**How am I implicated?**
*(This varies by person)*

**If you're Jewish:**
- How do I reconcile Jewish values with occupation?
- What does "safety for Jews" mean if it requires Palestinian suffering?
- How do I honor Holocaust memory while opposing genocide?

**If you're Arab/Muslim:**
- How do I process seeing Arab/Muslim lives devalued?
- What traditions of resistance can I draw on?
- How do I avoid despair?

**If you're American:**
- How does American exceptionalism blind me?
- What's my relationship to U.S. role in Middle East?
- How do I reckon with my country's complicity?

**What can I do?**
- Engage with cultural/religious leaders
- Draw on traditions of justice
- Create art, music, poetry
- Hold cultural events that build solidarity

**Example actions:**
```
→ Organize "Jews for Ceasefire" event
→ Host Palestinian cultural night
→ Write articles for cultural publications
→ Commission art about resistance and hope
```

### COUNTRY
**How am I implicated?**
- My taxes fund $3.8B/year to Israel
- My government provides weapons
- My elected officials enable this
- My country blocks UN resolutions

**What can I do?**
- Contact representatives
- Vote based on this issue
- Protest government policy
- Support political candidates who oppose funding

**Example actions:**
```
→ Call Senator weekly demanding ceasefire
→ Attend Congressional town halls
→ Protest at weapons manufacturer offices
→ Primary challenge to pro-war incumbent
→ Track voting records and share publicly
```

### GLOBAL/SYSTEMIC
**How am I implicated?**
- Colonial legacies created this situation
- Capitalism profits from military industry
- White supremacy devalues Palestinian lives
- Nation-state system enables occupation

**What can I do?**
- Support movements for systemic change
- Connect Palestine to other liberation struggles
- Challenge underlying systems
- Build alternative structures

**Example actions:**
```
→ Connect to BLM, Indigenous struggles, climate justice
→ Support international boycott movements
→ Challenge nation-state militarism
→ Build mutual aid networks
→ Envision post-colonial futures
```

## Example 2: Climate Crisis

### INDIVIDUAL
**How am I implicated?**
- My carbon footprint from lifestyle
- Consumption habits
- Transportation choices
- Energy use

**What can I do?**
- Reduce personal emissions
- Divest from fossil fuels
- Change consumption
- Learn climate science

**Actions:**
```
→ Switch to bike for commute
→ Move retirement fund to fossil-free index
→ Buy less, buy used
→ Take climate science course
```

### FAMILY
**How am I implicated?**
- Family wealth from extractive industries?
- Inherited consumption patterns
- Family land use
- Intergenerational responsibility

**What can I do?**
- Family climate conversations
- Change family investments
- Plan for climate adaptation
- Consider future generations

**Actions:**
```
→ Family meeting on investment strategy
→ Winterize parents' home (energy efficiency)
→ Plan for where family can live as climate changes
→ Create family climate action plan
```

### COMMUNITY
**How am I implicated?**
- Local infrastructure fossil fuel dependent
- Community zoning enables sprawl
- Local economy tied to extraction
- Lack of public transit

**What can I do?**
- Organize community solar
- Advocate for transit
- Support resilience projects
- Build local food systems

**Actions:**
```
→ Start community solar co-op
→ Petition for protected bike lanes
→ Community garden on vacant lot
→ Mutual aid network for extreme weather
```

### CULTURE
**How am I implicated?**
- Cultural attachment to cars (US)
- Consumerism as identity
- Disconnection from land
- Extraction as "progress"

**What can I do?**
- Challenge cultural narratives
- Revive land-based practices
- Create new stories
- Honor Indigenous knowledge

**Actions:**
```
→ Write about car culture critique
→ Learn from Indigenous land management
→ Create art about post-carbon futures
→ Host community storytelling events
```

### COUNTRY
**How am I implicated?**
- Highest per-capita emissions (if US)
- Government subsidizes fossil fuels
- Military is massive emitter
- Blocks international climate action

**What can I do?**
- Political organizing for Green New Deal
- End fossil fuel subsidies
- Push for climate reparations
- Support international cooperation

**Actions:**
```
→ Campaign for congressional candidates
→ Protest Exxon, Shell, etc.
→ Push for climate justice in foreign policy
→ Support Global South climate adaptation funding
```

### GLOBAL/SYSTEMIC
**How am I implicated?**
- Capitalism requires endless growth
- Colonialism set up extraction
- Global North wealth built on emissions
- Current system structurally unsustainable

**What can I do?**
- Support degrowth movements
- Climate reparations
- System transformation
- Build alternatives now

**Actions:**
```
→ Join ecosocialist organizing
→ Support Indigenous land back movements
→ Divest/blockade fossil fuel infrastructure
→ Build cooperative/commons alternatives
→ Participate in system redesign efforts
```

## Why This Matters

### Avoids Oversimplification
**Not:** "Donate $5 and feel better"
**Yes:** "Here's your actual relationship to this, at multiple scales"

### Enables Appropriate Action
Different people can act at different levels:
- Individual with limited time → Personal changes
- Community organizer → Local campaigns
- Political strategist → Legislative work
- Artist/writer → Cultural shift

### Shows Interconnection
- Individual action connects to systemic
- Family influences community
- Culture shapes country
- All levels matter

### Reduces Paralysis
**Not:** "I'm just one person, I can't do anything"
**Yes:** "I can act at multiple levels, each matters"

### Honors Complexity
**Not:** Simple villain/victim narratives
**Yes:** We're all implicated in systems, at different levels

### Builds Solidarity
Seeing how others engage at different levels:
- Validates your approach
- Offers new strategies
- Creates collective power

## Implementation

### Database Schema

```sql
CREATE TABLE implication_levels (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY,
  issue_id UUID REFERENCES issues(id),
  level TEXT, -- individual, family, community, culture, country, systemic

  -- Prompts
  implication_prompt TEXT, -- "How am I implicated?"
  action_prompt TEXT,      -- "What can I do?"

  -- Content (can be Pack-specific)
  pack_id UUID REFERENCES packs(id), -- null = general

  -- Examples
  example_implications TEXT[],
  example_actions JSONB,

  -- Resources
  learning_resources JSONB,
  action_templates UUID[] REFERENCES response_templates(id),

  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- Track what users are doing at each level
CREATE TABLE user_level_actions (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY,
  user_id UUID REFERENCES profiles(id),
  implication_level_id UUID REFERENCES implication_levels(id),
  action_taken TEXT,
  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW()
);
```

## Critical Nuance: Cultural Sensitivity

### This framework needs to be:

**1. Customizable**
Not everyone has the same levels:
- Some cultures prioritize family > individual
- Some people don't identify with nation-state
- Indigenous people have different relationships to "country"

**Solution:** Let Packs customize the levels

**2. Non-prescriptive**
Not saying "you SHOULD feel guilty at X level"
Just exploring "here's one way to think about it"

**Solution:** Frame as invitation, not obligation

**3. Intersectional**
Your position at each level depends on:
- Race, class, gender
- Location (Global North/South)
- Identity (oppressor/oppressed in different contexts)

**Solution:** Multiple perspectives at each level

## Open Questions

1. **Should levels be customizable per Pack?**
   - SF Pack focuses on Individual → Country
   - Manila Pack focuses on Family → Global

2. **How do we avoid "privilege guilt spirals"?**
   - Implication awareness → Action
   - Not: Implication awareness → Paralysis

3. **What if someone's implicated in harmful ways?**
   - Work for weapons manufacturer
   - Family wealth from extraction
   - How do we hold that without shame?

4. **How do we handle genuine disagreement?**
   - "My country's role is justified"
   - "This isn't actually genocide"
   - Do levels allow for that?
