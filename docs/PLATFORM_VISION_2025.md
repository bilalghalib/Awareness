## Two Content Types

### Events (Discrete)
- Earthquake in Turkey
- Aid convoy reaches Gaza
- Wildfire containment achieved
- Court ruling issued

**Characteristics:**
- Happened at specific time
- Verifiable as occurred/not occurred
- Has beginning and end
- Calls for immediate response

**Platform handles:**
- Verification trail
- Immediate Pack mobilization
- Response coordination
- Impact tracking

### Issues (Ongoing)
- Climate crisis
- Gaza humanitarian crisis
- Sudan war
- Homelessness in SF

**Characteristics:**
- No single start/end
- Multiple interpretations valid
- Requires sustained attention
- Calls for ongoing response

**Platform handles:**
- Collective sensemaking
- Sustained campaigns
- Long-term impact tracking
- Multiple perspectives

## Content Protection Framework

### Tier 1: Always Show (Default)
- Text descriptions
- Maps, charts, data
- Landscapes, locations
- People in life (not death)
- Community, connection

### Tier 2: Context (Opt-In)
- Historical background
- System analysis
- Before/after (buildings, not bodies)
- "What it means" discussions

### Tier 3: Evidence (Validators Only)
- Raw source material
- May include graphic content
- Behind warnings
- Not visible to Witnesses

### Memorial Approach
**Instead of:** Circulating victims' worst moments
**Show:** Lives lived, names spoken, stories told
**Enable:** Action that honors their dignity

**@eye.on.palestine + Awareness:**

1. They post breaking news (what they do best)

   - Full verification trail

   - Hope (visible impact)

4. We get:
   - Reach (their audience)
   - Relevance (current issues)
   - Trust (their credibility)
   - Purpose (enabling their work)

## Key Design Principles

### 1. Dignity Over Engagement
- Never show victims' worst moments
- Verification ≠ voyeurism
- Humanity > clicks
- Honor > exploit

### 2. Collective Over Individual
- No one holds everything alone
- Pack distributes load
- Shared sensemaking
- Coordinated action

### 3. Sustained Over Reactive
- Issues, not just events
- Campaigns, not just responses
- Long-term presence
- Visible progress over time

### 4. Multiple Over Single
- Many interpretations can coexist
- Respect disagreement
- Act despite difference
- No forced consensus

### 5. Bounded Over Infinite
- Clear entry and exit
- Closing rituals
- 1-2 Moments per week
- Protection from overwhelm

### 6. Action Over Paralysis
- Always have pathways
- See others responding
- Track collective impact
- Transform awareness → healing

## Critical Decisions Needed

### 1. Content Policy
**Question:** How graphic can content be?

**Options:**
A. Never show graphic content (dignity always)
B. Allow behind opt-in warnings (user choice)
C. Validator-only graphic content (protect most)

**Recommendation:** Option C
- Protects Maya's heart
- Enables Sara's work
- Respects agency

### 2. Interpretation Policy
**Question:** Does Awareness take positions on issues?

**Options:**
A. Neutral platform (just facts, no interpretation)
B. Multiple perspectives (show range of views)
C. Community consensus (Packs decide together)

**Recommendation:** Option B + C
- Show verified facts
- Show range of interpretations
- Each Pack does own sensemaking
- No platform-wide "truth" on meaning

### 3. Instagram Strategy
**Question:** How do we relate to Instagram aggregators?

**Options:**
A. Compete (try to replace them)
B. Aggregate (pull their content)
C. Partner (provide infrastructure)

**Recommendation:** Option C
- Partner with 2-3 trusted accounts
- Provide verification + coordination
- Revenue share model possible
- Win-win-win

### 4. Issue vs. Event Balance
**Question:** What's the primary focus?

**Options:**
A. Events only (original model)
B. Issues only (ongoing engagement)
C. Both (Events feed into Issues)

**Recommendation:** Option C
- Moments trigger attention
- Issues sustain engagement
- Events → Issues → Campaigns → Impact

### 5. Scale Strategy
**Question:** How big should Packs be?

**Options:**
A. Small (15-20 people, deep intimacy)
B. Medium (50-100 people, sustainable)
C. Large (500+ people, impact at scale)

**Recommendation:** Option A + Topic diversity
- 15-20 people per Pack (intimate)
- Many Packs per Issue (scale)
- Cross-Pack coordination (impact)
- Prevents overwhelm for individuals

## Business Model Questions

### Who Pays?

**Option A: Users (Subscription)**
- Pro: Sustainable revenue
- Con: Excludes those who can't afford

**Option B: Organizations (B2B)**
- Pro: Large revenue potential
- Con: Conflicts of interest possible

**Option C: Grants (Philanthropic)**
- Pro: Aligns with mission
- Con: Unsustainable long-term

**Option D: Hybrid**
- Free tier (basic awareness)
- Paid tier (advanced features)
- Institutional partnerships (schools, orgs)
- Grant funding (development)

**Recommendation:** Option D
- Free: Event feed, Pack membership, basic reflection
- Paid ($5/month): Advanced analytics, custom Packs, API access
- Institutional: White-label Packs, admin tools, training
- Grants: Core development, new features

### What's the North Star?

**NOT:** Monthly active users
**NOT:** Engagement time
**NOT:** Viral growth

**YES:** Sustained collective care metrics:
- People staying engaged >6 months
- Actions taken (not just views)
- Burnout rates (want them LOW)
- Cross-Pack coordination
- Issues resolved or improved
