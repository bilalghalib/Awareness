# Financial Sustainability Model
## Compensating Care Work & Building a Sustainable Platform

---

## Core Principle

**Awareness compensates the labor that makes collective care possible.**

The platform exists because people do work:
- **Validators** verify truth
- **Content partners** surface stories
- **Pack organizers** hold communities
- **Responders** coordinate action
- **Journalists** report from the ground

Currently, much of this work is unpaid or underfunded. Awareness should change that.

---

## Who Gets Paid & Why

### 1. Validators (Priority: High)

**Why compensate:**
- Emotional labor (exposure to difficult content)
- Skilled work (verification takes expertise)
- Time investment (thorough investigation)
- Trauma risk (need support resources)

**Compensation model:**
```
Base: $50/month stipend for active validators
Per-verification: $5-20 depending on complexity
Trauma support: $100/month therapy/counseling budget
Recognition: Public validator profile, impact stats

Example:
- Sara verifies 10 events/month (avg $10 each) = $100
- Base stipend = $50
- Therapy budget = $100
- Total: $250/month for ~15 hours of work
```

**Why this works:**
- Not a full-time salary (keeps it mission-driven)
- Recognizes emotional labor
- Makes validation sustainable
- Enables validators from all economic backgrounds

---

### 2. Content Partners (Instagram/TikTok Aggregators)

**Why compensate:**
- They do curation work we don't have to
- They have audiences we benefit from
- They take risks (harassment, account bans)
- Fair exchange of value

**Revenue share model:**
```
When partner drives users who subscribe:
- Partner gets 30% of subscription revenue for 12 months
- Awareness gets 70% for platform operations

Example:
- @eye.on.palestine drives 1,000 subscribers ($10/month)
- Monthly revenue: $10,000
- Partner gets: $3,000/month
- Awareness gets: $7,000/month
```

**Alternative: Direct grants**
```
Awareness gives partner organizations:
- $500-2,000/month unrestricted grants
- Based on impact, not just subscriber conversion
- Enables their continued work
```

**Hybrid approach (recommended):**
- Revenue share for subscriber conversions
- Plus base grant for partnership ($500/month)
- Plus bonus for high-quality moments ($50 per verified moment)

---

### 3. Pack Organizers (Future)

**Why compensate:**
- Holding space is emotional labor
- Organizing sustained action takes time
- Community care is real work
- Enables more Packs to form

**Compensation model (Phase 4+):**
```
Active Pack organizers (15+ members, 6+ months):
- $100/month stipend
- Bonus for retention ($5/member/month for members >6 months)
- Training and support budget
- Access to organizer network

Example:
- Pack of 18 people, 12 have been members >6 months
- Base: $100
- Retention bonus: 12 × $5 = $60
- Total: $160/month
```

---

### 4. Journalists & Reporters (Partnership Model)

**Why compensate:**
- Original reporting costs money
- Local journalists often underfunded
- Gaza/Sudan/etc reporters risk their lives
- Good journalism enables verification

**Model:**
```
Awareness doesn't commission journalism (we're not a news org)
BUT we can:
- Partner with journalist networks
- Revenue share when their stories drive engagement
- Provide grants to journalist collectives
- Highlight their work (traffic/recognition)

Example partnerships:
- Gaza journalists collective: $1,000/month grant
- Sudan reporter network: $500/month grant
- Climate investigative journalists: revenue share on stories
```

## Revenue Model (Detailed)

- Follow 3 Issues

- View verifications
- Reflections (pack-visible)
- Basic actions

**Goal:** Enable everyone to participate, regardless of ability to pay

### Paid Individual ($8/month or $80/year)

**"Sustained Engagement" tier**
- Follow unlimited Issues
- Join up to 3 Packs
- Advanced analytics (your impact over time)
- Custom notifications
- Private reflections
- Early access to new features
- Support the platform mission

**Why $8/month:**
- Affordable for most
- Covers operational costs
- Enables compensation pools
- Lower than news subscriptions

---

### Paid Pack ($15/month, split among members)

**"Collective Subscription"**
- Whole Pack subscribes together
- $15/month for Pack of 15 = $1/person/month
- Unlocks:
  - Custom Pack Issue focus
  - Pack analytics dashboard
  - Advanced coordination tools
  - Pack-specific action campaigns
  - Pack archive/memory

**Why this works:**
- More affordable per person
- Encourages Pack cohesion
- Aligns with collective values

## Critical Design Decisions

### 1. Should validators be paid per-verification or stipend?

**Option A: Per-verification ($5-20 each)**
- Pro: Scales with work
- Pro: Clear value exchange
- Con: Could create perverse incentives (quantity over quality)
- Con: Uneven income (some months more events than others)

**Option B: Monthly stipend ($200-300)**
- Pro: Stable income
- Pro: Recognizes ongoing readiness
- Con: Doesn't scale with actual work
- Con: Could enable free-riding

**Recommended: Hybrid**
- Base stipend ($50/month for being available)
- Per-verification bonus ($5-20 depending on complexity)
- Monthly cap to prevent burnout
- Therapy/support budget regardless of verification volume

---

### 2. Should Pack organizers be employees or volunteers?

**Employees:**
- Pro: Professionalized community care
- Pro: Accountability, training, standards
- Con: Expensive, limits growth
- Con: Changes the nature of Packs (feels corporate)

**Volunteers with stipends:**
- Pro: Maintains grassroots feel
- Pro: Scalable (many organizers)
- Pro: Stipend recognizes labor without professionalizing
- Con: Less control/consistency

**Recommended: Volunteers with stipends**
- Not employment, but recognition
- $100-200/month for active organizers
- Training and peer support network
- Can scale to 100+ organizers

---

### 3. Free tier vs. paywall

**Full paywall:**
- Pro: Maximizes revenue
- Con: Excludes people who can't pay
- Con: Contradicts mission (collective care for all)

**Fully free:**
- Pro: Maximum accessibility
- Con: Unsustainable without massive grant funding
- Con: No revenue from direct users

**Freemium (recommended):**
- Core features free (event feed, 1 Pack, basic actions)
- Premium features paid (multiple Packs, analytics, advanced tools)
- Pay-what-you-can option
- Scholarship program for those who can't afford

**Philosophy:**
- Inability to pay never excludes you from core mission
- Those who can pay subsidize those who can't
- Institutional clients subsidize individual users

## Implementation Phases

### Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Build Free Platform
- No monetization yet
- Prove the model works
- Build user base (target: 500-1,000 users)
- Apply for initial grants ($100K-250K)
- Fund through grants + founder capital

### Phase 2 (Months 7-12): Launch Paid Tiers
- Paid individual tier ($8/month)
- Institutional tier (first 2-3 clients)
- Begin validator stipends (5-10 validators)
- Begin partner grants (2-3 partners)
- Target: 200 paying individuals, 2 institutions

### Phase 3 (Months 13-18): Scale Compensation
- Pack organizer stipends
- Expanded validator program (20+ validators)
- More content partner grants (5-10 partners)
- Revenue share program with partners
- Target: 1,000 paying individuals, 5 institutions

### Phase 4 (Months 19-24): Sustainability
- Breakeven or profitable on operations
- Grants fund new features, not core operations
- 20+ compensated validators
- 10+ partner organizations
- 30+ Pack organizers with stipends
- Target: 2,000+ paying individuals, 10 institutions

## Alternative Models Considered

### Wikipedia Model (Donation-driven)
- Pro: Proven at scale
- Con: Requires constant fundraising campaigns
- Con: Volunteer-only model may not work for emotional labor

### Patreon Model (Creator subscriptions)
- Pro: Direct supporter relationship
- Con: Puts founder/team as "creators" not facilitators
- Con: Doesn't capture institutional value

### Cooperative Model (Member-owned)
- Pro: Aligns with values (collective ownership)
- Pro: Members govern the platform
- Con: Complex to set up and run
- Con: May slow decision-making

**Could adopt cooperative structure in Phase 4+**

## Ethical Considerations

### 1. Paying Validators Creates Incentives

**Risk:** Validators approve events to get paid
**Mitigation:**
- Multiple validators per event
- Reputation system
- Audit process
- Cap on monthly verifications (prevent burnout + gaming)

### 2. Revenue Share May Compromise Editorial

**Risk:** Partners post more to maximize revenue
**Mitigation:**
- Quality threshold (only verified moments count)
- Cap on monthly payouts
- Grant component independent of conversions
- Partnership agreements emphasize quality

### 3. Paid Tiers May Create Two-Class System

**Risk:** Free users are "second class"
**Mitigation:**
- Core features always free
- Pay-what-you-can option
- Scholarship program
- Premium features are conveniences, not core mission
